The practice site's 12 HTML pages are the originals, recovered from the SEO Lab page on avro.academy (`seo-lab-pages.js` is its `window.AVRO_SEO_PAGES`, with `{SITE}` placeholders). In the download, `before` is `broken/`, `after` is `fixed/`, and `{SITE}` is `https://your-username.github.io/bayside-paws/`.

Redrawn, not original: the images (`make_images.py`, same names and close to the original sizes, as listed in the lab data), `style.css`, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` (made the way the lab's generator makes them).

`make_templates.py` builds the two spreadsheets and two Word templates; `../checklist.html` and `../cheat.html` build the PDFs with `_tools/build_cheatsheet.py`.
