How Google works

CrawlGooglebot follows links and sitemaps
Indexstores and understands the page
Rankorders results for each search

Google rewards helpful, people-first pages and trust (E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust).

Search intent

Informationallearn: guide, blog post
Commercialcompare: services, reviews
Transactionalact now: booking page
Navigationalfind one business: home, contact

Search the keyword: what ranks shows the intent.

Keywords

  • Small businesses win with long-tail and local: "dog grooming Geelong West"
  • Free tools: autocomplete, People also ask, related searches, Google Trends, Keyword Planner, your Search Console
  • Keyword map: one main keyword per page, one page per main keyword

Titleunique, keyword first, brand last, 50–60 chars
Descriptionunique, benefit + detail + call to action, 120–160 chars

On the page

  • One H1; H2s for sections; real heading tags
  • Answer first, then detail; be specific
  • URLs: /blog/how-often-should-i-groom-my-dog.html
  • Link text says where it goes, not "click here"
  • Prices and hours as text, not images

Local SEO

  • Google Business Profile: real name, most specific category, hours, services, photos, posts
  • Map pack = relevance + distance + prominence
  • NAP identical everywhere: site footer, profile, directories
  • Directories: Yellow Pages, True Local, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect; India: Justdial, Sulekha

Reviews and links

  • Ask every customer, at the happy moment, with your review link
  • Reply to every review; calm and offline for bad ones
  • No fake reviews, incentives or review gating
  • Earn links: local news, partners, sponsorships, useful guides
  • Never buy links; mark paid ones rel="sponsored"

Content and AI

  • Ideas: keyword gaps, questions customers ask, Search Console page 2, seasons
  • No doorway pages (copy-paste suburb pages)
  • AI can outline and suggest; you add experience and check every fact
