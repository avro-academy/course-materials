About Bayside Paws

Bayside Paws Dog Grooming was started by Mia in 2019 after ten years as a vet nurse. We're a small, cage-free salon: every dog is groomed by hand, with no rushing.

Our team

Mia (owner, Certificate III in Companion Animal Services) and two qualified groomers. We're members of the Professional Groomers Association.

Find us at 14 Pakington Street, Geelong West VIC 3218, or call (03) 5550 0142. Contact and opening hours.
