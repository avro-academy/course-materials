Blog Post #7

When we opened in 2019 we had no idea how busy we would get! Our first customer was a very fluffy Samoyed called Snow who visited every Saturday. Since then we have groomed thousands of dogs and we have learned a lot. Our team loves coffee and we have a salon cat called Biscuit. We recently bought a new hydraulic table which makes grooming big dogs much easier and safer. We also changed our shampoo supplier to a local Victorian company. Anyway, people often ask us how often they should get their dog groomed. It depends on the dog really. Some need it more than others. Ask us next time you come in!
