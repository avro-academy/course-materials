Plan

  • Fits one content pillar and one goal (awareness, engagement or action)
  • On the content calendar, mixed with other pillars and formats this week
  • Right format for the job: Reel to reach new people, carousel for tips people save, photo for results, story for quick updates

Caption

  • Hook: the first line stops the scroll (a question, a surprising fact, a name)
  • Value: the tip, story or offer in a few short lines
  • One call to action: book, save, share, comment or tap the link
  • Sounds like the brand: warm, plain-spoken, calm, a little playful; never pushy
  • Short lines and white space; easy to read on a phone
  • Plain words people search for ("dog grooming Geelong")

Facts and honesty

  • Prices, dates, times, phone number and spelling checked
  • No claims you can't back up ("Geelong's best groomer")
  • No fake urgency ("only 2 spots left!") and no fake reviews
  • Paid or gifted partnership? Clear "Paid partnership" label or "#ad" / "Gifted by…" up front
  • AI-written text edited into your voice; AI "facts" checked; no AI image passed off as a real result

Picture or video

  • Good light, clean background, steady, at the dog's eye level
  • Right size: feed 1080 × 1350 (4:5), story / Reel 1080 × 1920 (9:16)
  • Video: best moment in the first 2 seconds; 7–30 seconds
  • Graphics: brand colours and Inter, big short text, one message, logo small in the bottom-right corner, margin round the edges
  • Music from the platform's commercial library, or royalty-free
  • Images are yours, licensed or used with permission

Permission and privacy

  • The owner said yes to posting their dog (and its name)
  • Any person shown (customer, child, staff) agreed to appear
  • Reposts asked first and credited ("📸 by @sarahandbiscuit")
  • No addresses, phone numbers or booking screens in shot

Accessible

  • Your own alt text describing the picture (not "dog" or hashtags)
  • Captions on any video with speech, auto-captions checked
  • Hashtags in CamelCase: #GeelongDogs
  • 1–3 emoji, never instead of words; no "font generator" text
  • Strong contrast between text and background; no fast flashing

Hashtags and links

  • 3–5 specific hashtags, local and topic mixed
  • Link tagged with UTM codes in lowercase (utm_source=instagram, utm_medium=social)
  • Bio link and booking page work

After you post

  • Reply to comments in the first hour, then within a day; use names
  • Answer messages like phone calls; never ask for card details in a DM
  • Note the post's results at the end of the month
