Batch: film during the week, write and schedule in one hour.

How often

  • Instagram: 3 feed posts a week (1+ video), stories most days
  • Facebook: 2–3 a week
  • Google Business Profile: 1 update a week
  • Consistency beats volume

Captions

Hook (first line) → value → one call to action.

  • Write like you talk; short lines
  • 1–3 emoji, never instead of words
  • 3–5 specific hashtags in CamelCase: #DogGroomingGeelong
  • Plain search words in the caption

Photos, video, graphics

  • Window light, clean background, eye level, steady
  • Video: best moment first 2 s, 7–30 s, subtitles, licensed music
  • Graphics: brand colours and fonts, big short text, one message, logo small in a corner
Feed4:5, 1080 × 1350
Story / Reel9:16, 1080 × 1920

Accessible and responsible

  • Own alt text: "A fluffy apricot cavoodle on a grooming table after a teddy-bear trim"
  • Captions on video; no flashing; strong contrast
  • AI: ideas and tidying yes; check facts; no fake "after" photos
  • Permission for people, pets and reposts; credit
  • No misleading claims, fake urgency or fake reviews (ACCC; India: consumer protection rules)

Community and complaints

  • Reply to every comment, fast in the first hour; use names
  • DMs = bookings: saved replies, away message, no card details
  • Pause → thank, sorry, offer to fix → take it offline → follow up
  • Leave fair criticism; hide spam, abuse, private info (screenshot first)
  • Partnerships: "Paid partnership", #ad or "Gifted by…" up front (ACCC, AANA; India: ASCI)
  • 2FA on; give staff their own access

Paid social

  • Pay when: clear offer, measurable result, profile looks good
  • Boost to start; Ads Manager for testing and tracking
  • Location first; adults; broad interests; warm custom audiences
  • $5–$15 a day for ~a week, end date set
  • Test one thing at a time
  • Cost per result = spend ÷ results

Measure

Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach

70 + 8 + 5 + 20 = 103; 103 ÷ 1,100 = 9.4%

  • Reach = accounts; views = times seen
  • Saves = useful; clicks, messages, bookings = results
  • Ask every customer "How did you hear about us?"
