Tick what you've already done. Work through the rest one at a time: each box makes it much harder for someone to get into your accounts.

Passwords and logins

☐My email account has a long passphrase: four or more random words, not used anywhere else
☐My banking, work and social media accounts each have their own passphrase (no reusing)
☐I use a password manager (built into my phone or browser, or a separate app)
☐Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is on for my email
☐MFA is on for banking, social media, myGov and work accounts
☐I never share an MFA code, with anyone (real organisations won't ask)
☐I've changed any password I know has been shared, written down or reused

Passphrase tip: make your own random words, not song lyrics, quotes or personal details. See cyber.gov.au for more.

Devices

☐My phone has a screen lock (PIN, passcode, fingerprint or face)
☐My laptop or computer has a password and locks automatically
☐Automatic updates are on for my phone, computer and apps
☐I lock my screen when I walk away (Windows key + L · Mac Ctrl + Cmd + Q)
☐I don't plug in USB sticks I don't recognise
☐I avoid banking or work logins on free public Wi-Fi (unless work has set up secure access)
☐My important files are backed up (cloud storage or an external drive)

Scams and phishing

☐I know the red flags: urgency, too good to be true, odd sender, links that don't match, requests for codes or payment, unusual requests from "the boss", attachments I wasn't expecting
☐I check links by hovering (or long-pressing) before I click
☐I contact organisations using a number or website I find myself, not the one in the message
☐I know job scams: paying for training or kits, "receive and transfer money", text-only interviews, asking for TFN or bank details too early
☐I know where to report: my manager or IT at work · scamwatch.gov.au · cyber.gov.au
Stop · Check · Report. Clicked something you shouldn't have? Change your password, call your bank if you shared financial details, and tell your manager or IT straight away. There's no shame: scammers are professionals.

Privacy at work

☐I only look at information I need for my job
☐I never share customer details with friends, family or online
☐I don't take photos or screenshots of customer or business information
☐Work files stay in work systems, not personal apps or devices
☐Printed personal information is shredded or securely disposed of
☐I don't paste private or confidential information into AI tools

My own privacy

☐I've checked the privacy settings on my social media accounts
☐I think before sharing my address, date of birth or TFN online
☐I know IDCARE (idcare.org) offers free help if my identity is stolen
