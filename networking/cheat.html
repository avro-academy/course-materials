Troubleshoot from the bottom up

1. LinkLights, cable, Wi-Fi connected?
2. AddressSensible IP? Not 169.254?
3. Localping the gateway
4. Beyondping 1.1.1.1 (by number)
5. Namesnslookup a website
6. AppAnother browser or site

Ask first: what, who, since when, what changed? Change one thing at a time. Write a ticket.

Wi-Fi and small networks

2.4 GHz: range · 5 GHz: speed · 6 GHz: fastest, shortest. Router high and central; mesh for big places. WPA3 (or WPA2-AES), long password, WPS off, guest network for visitors and smart devices, change the router admin password, update firmware, no remote management.
