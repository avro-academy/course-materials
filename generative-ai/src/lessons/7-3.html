AI without writing code

Not every AI tool needs a programmer. Low-code and no-code tools let people build apps and automations by dragging blocks and filling in forms. Many now include AI blocks: "summarise this", "sort this email", "pull out these details".

What people build with them

  • Automations — when a new enquiry form arrives, have AI sort it and pull out the student's name, year and subject, add a row to a spreadsheet, and email the right tutor
  • Document processing — read invoices or forms and pull out the amounts and dates
  • Simple chat assistants — answer questions from a set of documents, built from a template
  • Personal helpers — summarise the day's emails every morning
