# Prices and payment

| Lesson type | Length | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small group (up to 4 students) | 60 minutes | $45 per lesson |
| One-on-one | 60 minutes | $85 per lesson |
| HSC intensive (Year 12, small group) | 90 minutes | $70 per lesson |

Lessons are paid for each term, in advance, by bank transfer or card. A term of small-group lessons (10 weeks) costs $450.

The first lesson for a new student is a free trial. There is no enrolment fee.

Brothers and sisters get 10% off when both are enrolled in the same term.
