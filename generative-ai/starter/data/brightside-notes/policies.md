# Policies

## Missed lessons and cancellations
If you tell us at least 24 hours before a lesson that you can't come, we'll book a make-up lesson in the same term. With less than 24 hours' notice the lesson can't be made up or refunded, except for illness with a doctor's certificate.

## Refunds
If you leave part way through a term, we refund the lessons not yet taken, minus a $30 admin fee. Ask before week 8 of the term.

## Homework
Students get a short homework sheet after each lesson. It should take 20 to 30 minutes.

## Safety
Every tutor holds a current NSW Working With Children Check. Students under 12 must be collected by a parent or carer from the front desk.

## Phones
Phones stay in bags during lessons unless the tutor asks students to use them.
