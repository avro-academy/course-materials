# About Brightside Tutoring

Brightside Tutoring is a small tutoring centre at 14 Church Street, Parramatta NSW 2150. It opened in 2019 and is run by Priya Nair, a former high school maths teacher.

We tutor school students from Year 3 to Year 12 in maths, English and science. Lessons are in small groups of up to four students, or one-on-one.

Opening hours during school terms: Monday to Thursday 3:30 pm to 8:00 pm, Saturday 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. We are closed on Fridays, Sundays and public holidays.
