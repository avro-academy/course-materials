# Term dates 2027

Brightside runs four 10-week terms, close to the NSW school terms.

| Term | Starts | Ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Term 1 | Monday 1 February | Friday 9 April |
| Term 2 | Monday 26 April | Friday 2 July |
| Term 3 | Monday 19 July | Friday 24 September |
| Term 4 | Monday 11 October | Friday 17 December |

Enrolments for each term open four weeks before it starts. Holiday workshops run in the first week of each school holiday.
