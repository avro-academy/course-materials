Order Ahead lets Corner Café customers order from the menu on their phone or computer and collect at the counter. Payment is made at the counter, so it's not part of the app. Version 1.0.

IDAreaRequirement
REQ-1MenuThe menu shows every item with its name, category and price. Prices always show dollars and cents, like $4.80.
REQ-2CategoriesThe category buttons are All, Coffee, Tea, Drinks and Food. Each shows only that category's items: Coffee — Flat white, Latte, Cappuccino, Long black. Tea — English breakfast tea, Chai latte. Drinks — Orange juice, Iced chocolate. Food — Banana bread, Blueberry muffin, Ham and cheese toastie, Avocado toast.
REQ-3SearchSearch finds items whose name contains the words typed. Capitals don't matter, and spaces before or after the words are ignored. If nothing matches, it shows "No items match your search."
REQ-4OptionsCoffee and Tea items can be Regular or Large, with Full cream, Skim or Oat milk. Large adds $1.00. Oat milk adds $0.80. Food and Drinks have no options.
REQ-5QuantityEach item can be ordered in a quantity from 1 to 10. For anything else (0, 11, a decimal, empty) the app shows "Choose a quantity from 1 to 10." and nothing is added.
REQ-6Your orderYour order lists each item with its options, quantity and line total. Adding the same item with the same options again increases the quantity of that line, instead of adding a new line.
REQ-7Changing the orderIn Your order, the quantity of each line can be changed (1 to 10) and any line can be removed. The line total, subtotal, discount and total update straight away.
REQ-8Discount codeThe code STUDENT10 takes 10% off the subtotal. Capitals don't matter. Only one code can be used per order: applying it again shows "A discount code has already been applied." Any other code shows "That code isn't valid."
REQ-9TotalsYour order shows Subtotal, Discount and Total in dollars and cents. Total = Subtotal − Discount. Discounts are rounded to the nearest cent. Prices include GST.
REQ-10CheckoutGo to checkout opens the checkout form. If the order is empty, the app shows "Add something to your order first." and doesn't open the form.
REQ-11NameName is required: 2 to 40 characters, using letters, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes (for example O'Brien or Smith-Jones).
REQ-12Mobile numberMobile number is required: an Australian mobile number of 10 digits starting with 04. Spaces are allowed, for example 0412 345 678 or 0412345678.
REQ-13Pickup timeThe pickup time is chosen from 15-minute times from 7:00 am to 2:45 pm.
REQ-14NotesNotes are optional, up to 200 characters. A counter shows how many characters have been used, like 37/200.
REQ-15ConfirmationAfter Place order, the confirmation shows "Order placed", a new order number for every order, the customer's name, pickup time, items and total. Start a new order empties Your order.
REQ-16Accessibility and phonesEverything can be done with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline. Every form field has a visible label connected to it. All screens work at 360 px wide without scrolling sideways.
REQ-17SecurityText that customers type (name, notes, search, discount code) is always shown exactly as typed. It's never treated as HTML or code.
REQ-18ErrorsThe app works without errors appearing in the browser console during normal use.

Out of scope

  • Payment (made at the counter)
  • The café's kitchen screens and staff tools
  • Accounts and order history
