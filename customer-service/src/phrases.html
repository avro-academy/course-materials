When you don't know

"Good question, let me check for you."
"I'm still learning, so I'll grab someone who knows."

Never guess about ingredients, allergies, prices or warranties.

Feature → benefit: "This backpack is waterproof… so your laptop stays dry when you ride to school."

Refunds

"I'm sorry it's not working. Do you have your receipt or proof of purchase? I'll grab my manager so we can sort this out for you."

Allergies

"Let me check the ingredients with the chef."
"This doesn't contain nuts, but it's made in a kitchen that uses nuts."
When serving: "This is the nut-free one."

Never say "it should be fine".
