# Visiting the café

## Where we are
Corner Café is at 12 Example Street, Sydney NSW, on the corner of Example Lane. Phone: (02) 5550 0000.
The nearest train station is a 5-minute walk away. There is 2-hour street parking on Example Lane, and a paid car park on the next block.

## Inside and outside
There are 30 seats inside and 12 outside on the footpath. The café is step-free, with an accessible toilet.
Free Wi-Fi: ask at the counter for the password.

## Dogs
Dogs are welcome at the outside tables. We have water bowls, and dog treats at the counter. Only assistance dogs can come inside.
