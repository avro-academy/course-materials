# Orders and bookings

## Ordering ahead and paying
You can phone ahead to order takeaway coffee and food, and pick it up at the counter.
We take cards, phones, cash and our gift cards. There is no surcharge on cards.

## Catering and gift cards
We don't do catering. For a group of more than 8, please phone us at least a day ahead.
Gift cards: buy one at the counter, for any amount from $20. They last three years.
