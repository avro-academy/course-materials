# Allergies and diets

## Gluten
We have gluten-free bread for any breakfast or toastie, for $2 extra. The orange and almond cake is gluten-free.
The banana bread is not gluten-free. Our kitchen uses flour, so if you have coeliac disease, please talk to our staff before you order.

## Vegetarian and vegan
The smashed avocado, granola, pumpkin salad and soup of the day are vegetarian. Ask for the smashed avocado without feta and egg to make it vegan.
The ham and cheese toastie is not vegetarian. We can make a cheese and tomato toastie instead.

## Nuts and other allergies
Some of our cakes contain nuts, and we use nuts in the kitchen.
If you have a serious allergy, please talk to our staff before you order, so they can check every ingredient with you. Café Helper can't check ingredients for allergies.
