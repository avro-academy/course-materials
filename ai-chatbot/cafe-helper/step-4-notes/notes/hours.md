# Opening hours

## Every week
Monday to Friday: 7am to 3pm.
Saturday: 8am to 2pm.
Sunday: 8am to 1pm.

## The kitchen
Breakfast is served until 11:30am. Lunch is served from 11:30am until 30 minutes before closing.
Coffee, cakes and drinks are available all day.

## Public holidays
We are closed on Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year's Day and Good Friday.
On other public holidays we open 8am to 1pm. Check our Instagram, @cornercafesyd, for changes.
