Three numbers that tell the story

=SUMIFS(…,Transactions!E$2:E$263,
        "One-off")
# current staff:
=COUNTIFS(Staff!A2:A1000,"<>",
          Staff!E2:E1000,"")
=IF(B11=0,"",B7/B11)    per person
=ROUND(B7/11,2)         GST inside

GST in a GST-inclusive price is ÷ 11, not × 10%. Compare per person with last year, not an "industry average".

Finding every subscription

  • Bank statement: anything that repeats
  • Every company card (where shadow IT hides)
  • Email: "invoice", "receipt", "renewal"
  • Single sign-on apps in the admin centre
  • Ask: "what do you use every week?"

Shadow IT: ask, don't accuse. Keep it (business plan, owner, register), replace it, or cancel it.

Licences: who holds each seat

=IF(COUNTIFS(Staff!A:A,B2,
             Staff!E:E,"<>")>0,
  "Left the company",
  IF(D2>90,"Not used in 90+ days",
     "Active"))

Keep a leaver's files and mail first. "Not used" isn't always waste: ask. Annual plans shrink only at renewal.

Cut the waste

  • Unused seats and leavers' seats
  • Overlap: check what your big plan already includes (Teams, OneDrive)
  • Right-size: single-app plans, free viewer seats, lower tiers
  • Clean up first, then commit to annual billing
  • Never count a saving twice
  • Don't cut backups and security: false savings

Cancelling safely

Export everything (shared and deleted files too) · tell people · update links · overlap a week or two · cancel and record it · check the next statement. Check notice periods; never cancel the only copy.

Total cost of ownership

TCO = price + extra warranty + setup
    + repairs per year × years
    + monthly fee × 12 × years
    − resale
Cost per year = TCO ÷ years in use

Also count lost time, downtime and staff happiness.

Buy, lease or subscribe

Buylowest cost if kept long; a depreciating asset
Leasesmooth cash flow, repairs included; read the exit and damage terms
Subscribechoose the plan and seat count carefully

Quotes and negotiating

  • Write the spec first; same spec to everyone
  • 3 written quotes above your limit
  • Compare the real total: warranty, setup, delivery, GST
  • Ask at renewal; mention real alternatives; try resellers
  • Check the IT support contract every year

The budget

  • One line per cost, a reason in the notes
  • Contingency 5–10%
  • Plan for price rises; spread by month
  • Replace devices on a schedule, not all at once
  • Budget vs actual every month

To the boss: answers first, round numbers, a clear ask, honest trade-offs, the workbook attached. A sorted bar chart, never 3D.

Tax basics (check the ATO)

  • GST credits need a tax invoice over $82.50; a bank statement isn't enough
  • Subscriptions and repairs: expenses. Laptops: assets, depreciated
  • Instant asset write-off: check the current limit and dates
  • Ask a registered tax or BAS agent

Every month (15 minutes)

Categorise last month · anything new or dearer? · budget vs actual · renewals in the next 60 days · starters and leavers.

When someone leaves

Get the date · block sign-in · collect devices · keep their files and mail · remove every seat (filter Licences by name) · wipe the laptop · check the next bill.
