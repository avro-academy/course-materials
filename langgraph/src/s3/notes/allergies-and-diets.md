# Allergies and special diets

## Vegetarian and vegan
Everything on the menu is vegetarian except the ham and cheese toastie.
The veggie wrap is vegan if you ask for it without the yoghurt dressing. Any coffee can be made vegan with oat, soy or almond milk.

## Gluten
We have gluten-free bread for toasties and wraps for $1.50 extra. The banana bread, muffins and lemon slice contain gluten.
Our kitchen is small and uses wheat flour, so we can't promise any food is completely free of gluten. We don't recommend our food for people with coeliac disease.

## Nuts and other allergies
The lemon slice and the banana bread may contain traces of nuts. Almond milk contains nuts.
If you have a serious allergy, please tell the staff at the counter before you order, so they can check the ingredients with the kitchen.
