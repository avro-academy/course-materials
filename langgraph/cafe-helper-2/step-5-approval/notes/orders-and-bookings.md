# Orders, bookings and payments

## Booking a table
Book a table on our website or by phoning (02) 5550 0000. Online bookings are for 1 to 8 people, every half hour from 8am to 1pm.
For a group of more than 8, please phone us at least a day ahead.

## Paying
We take cards, phones and cash. There is no surcharge on cards.
You can phone ahead to order takeaway, and pick it up at the counter.

## Catering and gift cards
We don't do catering.
Gift cards are sold at the counter, for $20 to $100. They can be used for food and drinks, and are valid for three years.
