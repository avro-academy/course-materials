Your first payslip

Your employer must give you a payslip within one working day of paying you. Check it against your own record of hours.

If something doesn't seem right: check fairwork.gov.au (Pay Calculator), ask your supervisor politely ("Could we check my pay together?"), or call the Fair Work Ombudsman on 13 13 94. If you're unsafe or being harassed, tell a manager you trust or your state's work health and safety regulator.

General information only. Rules and amounts change: check ato.gov.au and fairwork.gov.au for current details.
