Start every resume bullet point with a strong action verb, then say what you did and the result. Pick verbs that match the skills in the job ad.

The formula: Action verb + what you did + result or detail
"Handled cash and EFTPOS for up to 80 customers per shift with no errors."

Prove your soft skills

Everyone says "I'm reliable". Back every skill up with a short example from your life audit.

SkillProve it like this
Reliability"I didn't miss a single training session or game all season."
Customer service"Served up to 80 customers on busy match days at the club canteen."
