The recording passage (Day 1 and Final)

Hi, this is Jordan from Corner Café. Thanks for calling back. Your order for three vegetarian wraps, two large flat whites and a fresh fruit salad will be ready at twelve fifteen on Thursday. The total is forty-seven dollars and thirty cents. We've also got lemon slices and raspberry muffins this week, if you'd like to add something extra. Just ask for me — that's J-O-R-D-A-N. Our address is 16 Victoria Street, near the library. If anything changes, ring us on 0412 345 678. Have a really great day!

How to record

  1. Find a quiet room. Use your phone's voice recorder or camera app.
  2. Hold the phone about 20–30 cm from your mouth.
  3. Read the passage once silently, then record it at your normal speed. Don't try to be perfect.
  4. Save it as "Clear speech — Day 1". At the end of the course, record it again as "Clear speech — Final".

Workplace scripts

Script 1: Customer at the counter

Hi there, what can I get for you? … Sure — one large flat white with oat milk and a toasted cheese sandwich. That's fourteen fifty. Tap whenever you're ready. … Thanks! It'll be about five minutes. I'll call out your name.

Script 2: Taking a phone message

Good morning, Westfield Pharmacy, this is Priya. … She's not available right now — can I take a message? … Could you spell your surname for me? … Thanks, that's N for November, G for Golf, U for Uniform, Y for Yankee, E for Echo, N for November. And your number? … So that's 0423 816 590. I'll make sure she gets it this afternoon.

Script 3: Interview introduction

Hi, I'm Ali. Thanks for having me. I'm in Year 12 at Parramatta High, and for the past two years I've volunteered at my local sports club canteen, where I've served customers on busy match days. I'm really keen to start my first retail job, and I'd love to work somewhere that cares about great customer service.

Script 4: Team briefing

Quick update before we open. Deliveries arrived at seven thirty, so the drinks fridge is restocked. The specials today are vegetable soup and lemon slice. We're short-staffed this afternoon, so if you finish your tasks early, please check with Mel. Thanks, everyone!

What to listen for

  • Numbers and names: would a stranger write them down correctly?
  • Endings: tasks, deliveries, restocked, arrived
  • Tricky sounds: th, v/w, r/l, clusters
  • Chunking: a tiny pause between ideas
