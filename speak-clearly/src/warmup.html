Do this before a shift, an interview or a presentation. Be gentle: stop anything that hurts, and skip the jaw exercises if you have jaw problems.

1. Jaw · 1 minute

  • Gently massage your cheeks in small circles, just in front of your ears.
  • Slowly drop your jaw open as if yawning, then close. Repeat 5 times.

2. Lips · 1 minute

  • Lip trills: blow air through loosely closed lips so they flutter ("brrrr"), like a motorbike. 3 times.
  • Smile and pout: wide smile ("ee"), then push your lips forward into a small circle ("oo"). Repeat 10 times.

3. Tongue · 1 minute

  • Stick your tongue out and down, then up toward your nose. 5 times.
  • Touch each corner of your mouth with your tongue tip. 5 times each side.
  • Run your tongue around the outside of your teeth, behind your lips, in a circle. 3 each way.

4. Voice · 1 minute

  • Hum "mmmm" gently on a comfortable note. Feel the buzz on your lips.
  • Slide your voice up and down on "ng" or "oo", like a siren.

5. Sound drill · 1 minute

Say each line clearly, slowly first, then faster:

ma – may – me – my – mo – moo
pa – ta – ka, pa – ta – ka, pa – ta – ka
red lorry, yellow lorry (×3)

Before you speak: breathe low

Hand on your stomach, breathe in slowly through your nose (your stomach moves out), breathe out on a gentle "ssss". 5 times. Then remember: open · finish · slow down.
