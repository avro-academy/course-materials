# Opening hours

## Every week
Monday to Friday: 7am to 3pm.
Saturday and Sunday: 8am to 2pm.
The kitchen stops taking food orders at 1pm every day. Coffee is served until closing.

## Public holidays
Most public holidays: 8am to 2pm, like a weekend.
Anzac Day (25 April): open from 10am to 2pm.
Closed on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day.

## Table bookings
Tables can be booked every half hour from 8am to 1pm, for 1 to 8 people, up to 60 days ahead.
Bigger groups: phone (02) 5550 0000.
